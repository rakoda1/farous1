About Us

Marine & electromechanical specialists

Rakoda for Marine & Electro Mechanical Works was established in 2008 by Dr. Eng. Raafat Aboabdou and his associates. We work with both the public and private sectors, covering marine construction, harbors and yacht marinas, as well as power, air conditioning, firefighting and pumping systems.

Our own workshop, heavy equipment and technical team let us design, fabricate, install and maintain on site — from Alexandria to the Red Sea, with projects such as the Safaga 1000-ton syncrolift and Safaga fishing port.

"Proven competent, efficient and reliable — earning the confidence of our clients since 2008."

🏗️

Marine Steel Structures

Design, installation and maintenance of marine steel constructions and marine pier equipment.

⚓

Harbors & Yacht Marinas

Marine construction and infrastructure for harbors and marinas, plus modular pontoons and upgrading of floating units.

⚡

Power & Air Conditioning

Supply, installation and maintenance of solar and diesel power stations and central air conditioning stations.

Firefighting & Pump Stations

Pumps, Compressors & Networks

Piling & Hydraulic Equipment
